Financial Literacy for High School Students

Building smart money habits early

Why Financial Literacy Matters

Financial literacy helps students understand how to budget, save, use credit wisely, and plan for the future. Without these skills, teens are more likely to struggle with debt and poor financial decisions as adults.

Core Financial Topics

Budgeting

Tracking income and expenses helps students stay in control of their money.

Saving & Emergency Funds

Saving prepares students for unexpected expenses and future goals.

Credit & Debt

Understanding interest rates and credit scores prevents long-term debt.

Investing Basics

Investing allows money to grow over time through compound interest.
