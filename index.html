Why Financial Literacy Matters

Financial literacy helps students make informed decisions about money before they face real financial responsibilities. Without these skills, many young adults struggle with debt, poor credit, and financial stress. Learning early builds independence, confidence, and long term stability.

Core Financial Concepts

Understanding these concepts can prevent major financial mistakes later in life.

Budgeting

Why it matters: Budgeting helps you control your money instead of losing track of it.

How to do it: Track income, list expenses, and limit unnecessary spending.

If you don’t: You may overspend and struggle to save.

Saving

Why it matters: Savings protect you from unexpected expenses.

How to do it: Save a portion of every paycheck.

If you don’t: You may rely on debt when problems happen.

Credit

Why it matters: Credit affects loans, housing, and major purchases.

How to manage it: Pay balances fully and avoid unnecessary debt.

If you don’t: Your credit score may drop.

Investing

Why it matters: Investing helps your money grow over time.

How to start: Learn basics like stocks and long term growth.

If you don’t: You may miss wealth building opportunities.
