Why Financial Literacy Matters

Financial literacy helps students make informed decisions before they face real financial responsibilities. Without these skills, many young adults struggle with debt, poor credit, and financial stress. Learning early builds independence, confidence, and long term stability.

Core Financial Concepts

Budgeting

Helps control spending and plan for needs.

Saving

Protects against emergencies and future expenses.

Credit

Impacts loans, housing, and financial opportunities.

Investing

Allows money to grow over time.

Income

Understanding earnings helps manage money better.

Taxes

Knowing how taxes work prevents financial confusion later.

Financial Planning

Helps set goals and prepare for the future.
